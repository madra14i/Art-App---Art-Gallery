Fernando Amorsolo

1892 - 1972 • National Artist for Visual Arts

Fernando Amorsolo

Nicknamed the "Grand Old Man of Philippine Art," he was the first-ever to be recognized as a National Artist of the Philippines. Fernando Amorsolo Y. Cueto was a portraitist and painter of rural Philippine landscapes.

Himala

Himala ('Miracle') is a 1982 Philippine religious drama film directed by Ishmael Bernal and produced by the Experimental Cinema of the Philippines. It stars Nora Aunor as a young woman living in the province who claims to have seen a Marian apparition.

Inay

Inay is a 1993 Filipino drama film directed by Artemio Marquez. The film stars Nora Aunor in the title role, along with Tirso Cruz III and Maridin Lumano.
